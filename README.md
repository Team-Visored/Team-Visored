## ◈ Find what they missed.

We follow the weird request, the loose permission, and the line of code that does not belong.

**Team Visored** is a cybersecurity and CTF team from Nepal, driven by curiosity and the satisfaction of understanding how things break. We explore unfamiliar systems, question assumptions, and turn difficult challenges into lessons worth sharing.

> **Look closer. Think deeper. Capture the unseen.**

## ◈ Where curiosity takes us

| Discipline | What we explore |
|:-----------|:----------------|
| **Web Security** | Application logic, access controls, and unexpected attack paths |
| **Cryptography** | Mathematical weaknesses, broken assumptions, and hidden structure |
| **Binary Exploitation** | Memory corruption, program behavior, and exploit development |
| **Reverse Engineering** | Following execution until the mystery becomes a mechanism |
| **Digital Forensics** | Recovering evidence from files, memory, and network traffic |
| **OSINT** | Connecting public clues and finding the detail that matters |
